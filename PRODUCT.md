# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

GitHub profile README: GitHub-flavored markdown plus hand-made SVG images committed to this repository. GitHub strips CSS and scripts from markdown, so all visual design lives inside the SVG files, with light and dark variants selected through `<picture>` and `prefers-color-scheme`.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers who land on github.com/klaygomes and need to judge, within seconds, who Cleiton is, how senior he is, and how to reach him. Engineers arriving from articles or repositories are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

The profile is a front page for Cleiton Loiola: Tech Lead at Qred in Stockholm, 20+ years of JavaScript and Node.js on UNIX, C and STM32 microcontrollers on weekends. Success is a visitor who understands his role and range, opens a project or article, and contacts him.

## Positioning

He works the whole stack, from React on the screen down to the clock tree of an STM32, and writes about each layer. His resume can be read in a terminal (`ssh cleiton.se`) and is typeset in groff.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Markdown cannot carry CSS. Images cannot hold multiple links; each image links to one URL at most.
- SVG rendered through GitHub's image proxy cannot load external fonts or scripts. Fonts must be embedded or converted to outlines.
- All text links in the current README must stay reachable as real links.

## Brand Commitments

- Blog name: Esta couve flor (estacouveflor.com).
- Products: TinyTroop, hurl-orchestra.
- Contact: email@cleiton.dev, LinkedIn klaygomes, `ssh cleiton.se`.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md copy only: role, employer, years of experience, projects, articles, links. No photos, logos, metrics, testimonials, or employer history beyond Qred. None of these may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Role, seniority, and contact are legible in the first screen.
2. Range is shown, not claimed: the projects span UI to microcontroller.
3. Every link still works as plain text for screen readers and copy-paste.
